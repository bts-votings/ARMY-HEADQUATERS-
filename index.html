How Voting Works

Hearts / stars / tickets: most apps give free daily votes that reset at a fixed time; some allow bonus votes via missions.

Account-based voting: most official apps require sign-in so votes are counted fairly.

Daily vs one-time: some shows allow one vote per round; others allow daily voting until the period ends.

Fanchants

Fanchants are the call-and-response member-name chants ARMY does live during songs. Basic pattern below — exact timing varies per song, so listen to a fanchant guide video for the specific track before a concert or event.

The basic full chant (7 members)

RM! Jin! Suga! J-Hope! Jimin! V! Jungkook! BTS!

Common intro chant

방탄소년단! (Bangtan Sonyeondan!)

Used at the start of many songs and stage intros.

Tips for chanting live

Listen for the beat cue before each member's name — fanchants are timed to specific instrumental gaps. Many fan channels post song-specific fanchant guide videos ahead of comebacks; check the official fanbase accounts for your country before an event.

A re-released album with bonus tracks.
Bias

FAQ
Is this an official BTS or HYBE site?

No. ARMY HEADQUARTERS is an unofficial fan-made dashboard. It only links out to official sources.

How do I support BTS legitimately as a beginner?

Stream on official platforms, buy from official retailers, vote through official apps, and follow verified accounts — everything you need is linked here.

Is my display name an account?

No — it's stored only in your browser's local storage on this device. There's no server, login, or password.
