Meet the Group

Who is BTS?

BTS (방탄소년단) is a seven-member group from Seoul, South Korea, formed by BIGHIT (now HYBE) and debuted in 2013. They write and produce much of their own music, and their fandom is called ARMY.

Learn the Lingo

Fandom Basics

A few essential terms to know before you dive in.

ARMY
The official fandom name — stands for "Adorable Representative M.C. for Youth," and it's also a play on how the group and fans go together.
Borahae (보라해)
"I purple you" — coined by V, meaning trust and love that lasts.
OT7
Supporting all seven members equally, not just a favorite.
Bias
Your favorite member. No pressure to pick one right away!
Weverse
The official platform where BTS post updates and communicate with ARMY directly.
Get Involved

How to Support BTS

The real, effective ways ARMY shows up for BTS — no gatekeeping, start wherever you can.

01

🗳️ Vote

Voting on award show and chart platforms genuinely helps BTS's recognition. Use Voting HQ to track ongoing votes and how to cast them.

Open Voting HQ ↗
02

🎧 Stream

Streaming on official platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube) is one of the most impactful things ARMY does. Use Streaming HQ for guides and party links.

Open Streaming HQ ↗
03

💰 Buy Official

Buying albums and merch through official stores (Weverse Shop, HYBE Insight) directly supports the group and counts toward chart sales.

04

📢 Engage Respectfully

Follow official accounts, engage on Weverse, and share content from verified sources — always credit and avoid spreading unverified rumors.

05

🎪 Join Fan Projects

Cupsleeve events, birthday projects, and charity drives are a fun, low-pressure way to connect with local ARMY.

06

🚫 Avoid

Don't buy from unofficial resellers, don't participate in mass-reporting wars, and don't share private/leaked content — it can hurt BTS more than help.
